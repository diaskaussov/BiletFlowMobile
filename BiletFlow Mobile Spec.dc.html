08

Copy & language

ElementRule
Verdict wordCaps, one or two words, never a sentence: VALID · ALREADY USED · REFUNDED TICKET · WRONG EVENT · CODE NOT RECOGNIZED · PROMOTIONAL CODE.
ExplanationOne sentence, 13–14 pt sentence case, states the fact and the consequence. No apologies, no exclamation marks.
Next stepEvery refusal offers at least one action: scan again, find by name, or open the record.
ButtonsCaps, verb first, 1–3 words. The scan-ending action always says what happens next, not "OK".
NumbersThin space as thousands separator (1 043). Times 24-hour, seconds only on result screens.
LocaleLayouts hold Kazakh and Russian at up to +35% string length; verdict words are the constrained case and were sized for it.
